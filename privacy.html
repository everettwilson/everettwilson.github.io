Grifter Privacy Policy

Last updated October 26, 2026

Grifter is a gift-planning app for small groups such as families. This policy explains what Grifter stores, who can see it, and how to delete it. Grifter is made by an independent developer and isn't affiliated with any company.

What Grifter stores

  • Your account: your email address and the display name you choose. If you sign in with Apple, Grifter gets the email and name Apple shares, which can be a private relay address that hides your real one. If you sign up with email, your password is stored only as a secure hash.
  • Groups: the names of groups you create, which groups you're in, when you joined, and whether you're the group's admin.
  • Messages: what you write in gift-planning threads. Some messages are posted for you automatically, such as when you claim a gift or join with someone's invite.
  • Wish lists: the titles, notes, links and price estimates you add.
  • Claims: which wish list items you've said you'll get.
  • Kids: any group member can add a child (or anyone without a phone) to a group. Grifter then stores the name they enter and the wish list they keep for the child. A child profile has no login, email or other contact details.
  • Invites: each invite link is a single-use code. Grifter records who created it and who used it. You send the link yourself, through Messages or any app you choose, so Grifter never sees who you sent it to.
  • Reports and blocks: if you report a message or a person, Grifter stores your reason, any details you add, a copy of the reported message, and who reported whom. If you block someone, Grifter stores that you blocked them. The person you blocked is not told.
  • Notifications: if you allow notifications, Grifter stores your device's push token so it can notify you of new messages. Notifications say only who sent a message, never what it says.
  • Read status: when you last opened each thread, so the app can show which ones have something new.

Like most online services, the servers keep short-term technical logs of requests, such as IP addresses and times, to keep the service running and secure.

Grifter does not collect your location, contacts, photos, phone number, or payment details. It has no ads, no analytics, and no third-party tracking.

Who can see it

  • Your messages, wish list and display name are visible only to people who share a group with you.
  • Every thread is hidden from the person it's about. You can't see the thread about you, its messages, or who has claimed items on your wish list. This is enforced by the database itself, not just by the app's screens.
  • A child's thread is visible to every member of their group, because the child has no account.
  • If you block someone, you stop seeing their messages and wish list.
  • Reports are visible only to the developer, who reviews them to enforce the Terms of Use.

Grifter does not sell your data or share it with advertisers or data brokers.

Service providers

Grifter uses a few companies to run the service. They handle data only to provide that service:

  • Supabase runs Grifter's database and sign-in, on Amazon Web Services in the United States. All the data above is stored there.
  • Apple delivers push notifications, and handles Sign in with Apple if you use it.
  • Resend may be used to email reports to the developer so they get a quick response. That email contains the report.
  • GitHub hosts this website and the pages invite links open.

Deleting your data

  • Delete your account at any time from the account menu on the Groups screen. This permanently deletes your account, your messages, your wish list, your claims, your invites, and your notification tokens. It also deletes any group you created, along with every thread and message in it. Child profiles you added stay in their group, and the group's admin can manage them. Deleting your account can't be undone.
  • Leave a group from its group details. The thread about you in that group is deleted. Messages you wrote in other people's threads stay until you delete your account.
  • Remove a child you added from the group details, which deletes their profile and wish list.
  • Reports are kept after an account is deleted, so the developer can still act on abuse. The deleted account's identity is removed from the report.

To ask a question, request a copy of your data, or have something deleted, email snugger-symptom0r@icloud.com.

Children

Accounts are for people 13 and older. Grifter doesn't knowingly let children under 13 create an account. If you believe a child has, email snugger-symptom0r@icloud.com and the account will be deleted. Child profiles added by a group member (see Kids above) hold only a name and a wish list, entered by that member.

Changes

If Grifter starts collecting something new or uses data in a new way, this policy and its date will be updated before the change takes effect.
