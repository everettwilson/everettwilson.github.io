🎁 Grifter Support

Questions, problems, or something to report? Email snugger-symptom0r@icloud.com and you'll hear back within a day or two.

Why can't I see the thread about me?

That's the point. Everyone in a group gets a thread that all the other members can see, so they can plan your gift without you knowing. You'll never see the thread about you, or who has claimed things on your wish list.

How do I join a group?

Ask someone in the group to send you an invite from the group's details (Invite Someone). Install Grifter, sign in, then tap the link in their message. Each invite works once, so ask for a new one if yours has been used.

Can I add someone who doesn't have a phone?

Yes. In a group's details, tap Add a Kid. You keep their wish list from the Wish List tab, and everyone in the group can plan their gifts.

How do I report or block someone?

Long-press a message, or tap the ⋯ next to a person in the group's details. Reports are reviewed within 24 hours. The person you block isn't told.

How do I delete my account?

On the Groups screen, tap the account icon, then Delete Account. This permanently deletes your account and data, including any groups you created. See the Privacy Policy for exactly what's removed.
