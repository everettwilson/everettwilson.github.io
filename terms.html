Grifter Terms of Use

Last updated October 21, 2026

Using Grifter

Grifter helps groups of family and friends plan gifts together. By creating an account or signing in, you agree to these terms. If you don't agree, don't use Grifter.

Zero tolerance for objectionable content

Grifter has no tolerance for objectionable content or abusive users. You must not post anything that harasses, bullies, threatens, or demeans another person; hate speech or slurs; sexual content; spam; or anything illegal. This applies to messages, wish list items, display names, and group names.

Reporting and blocking

You can report any message or group member, and block anyone so you no longer see their messages or wish list. Long-press a message, or tap the ⋯ button next to a member in the group's details, to find these options. Reports are reviewed within 24 hours.

What happens when someone breaks these terms

Content that breaks these terms is removed, and the account that posted it may be suspended or deleted, without notice.

Your account

You're responsible for what you post. You can delete your account at any time from the account menu on the Groups screen, which permanently deletes your messages, wish list, and any groups you created.

Changes

These terms may change as Grifter grows. Continuing to use Grifter after a change means you accept the new terms.
